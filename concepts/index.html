Portfolio concepts

One page per research direction, each built around a single thing you can play with. This index is a working list for review, not a published page.

  1. Anagram TilesHis name as letter tiles that rearrange into its real anagrams.words and tools
  2. One SplatHis name drawn with Gaussian splats, on a dial from 1 to 16,384.computer graphics
  3. SpecimenA neuron of 4,096 splats that you focus and fire.scientific visualization
  4. Toy BoxHis name as a splat toy to spin, poke, paint and drop.digital media
  5. WorkshopA drafting sheet where the build loop stops for you.software engineering
  6. OverprintOne cloud of splats, printed in two inks, that re-forms for every section.media arts
  7. MoleculeA chalk caffeine molecule you turn in your hand and measure, atom to atom.molecular visualization
  8. ResonanceA brass plate you bow, and black sand that runs to the still lines of the note.sound
  9. SliceTwo real CT scans, a walnut and a young gar's head, to cut, window and turn as a shaded cutaway.volume imaging
  10. AtlasOne plate that zooms from the Milky Way to a ribosome.scale and astronomy

A concept opens here once its branch is merged. The registry, with what is proposed next, is in docs/CONCEPTS.md.
